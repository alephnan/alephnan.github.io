01 // The experiment

A pattern that finds
its way back.

A shared update rule turns local information into a recognizable whole. The same rule continues running after damage.

Recorded inference

From a single cell to a beetle. Each cell reads its neighbors, then applies the same small neural network. Shape emerges over 384 recurrent updates.

A // Learning

Train for persistence.

A pool of 1,024 states teaches the model to keep a pattern alive. Fresh seeds and partially damaged states appear in training, so growth and repair share one rule.

B // Constraint

Life needs a neighbor.

A cell survives only when its 3 × 3 neighborhood contains sufficient alpha before and after the update. A completely erased grid stays empty until explicitly reseeded.

Recovery criterion

Erase 10–30% of foreground, then recover ≥90% of pre-damage IoU within 384 further updates.

Inspect the training curve +

Original experiment figure. Adam learning rate was reduced after update 2,000; training stopped when validation acceptance passed.

Limits // What this doesn’t prove

This is one model trained on one artwork. Six local repair cases failed. The first evaluation missed the repair target by one case; training continued and the final evaluation used fresh seeds. Half-erasure is a separate stress test, not an acceptance claim.
